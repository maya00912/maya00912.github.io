Model

Hydrogen-like orbitals ψnlm = Rnl(r)·Ylm(θ,φ). Each subshell's effective nuclear charge Zeff comes from Slater's screening rules. Each dot is one random sample of an electron position drawn from |ψ|². A filled subshell is spherically symmetric (Unsöld's theorem), so it is shown as "all m". Partly filled subshells can show a single real orbital. Distances are in Bohr radii (a₀ = 52.9 pm).
