How it's drawn

Atom positions come from published lattice constants and site coordinates. Density and packing fraction are calculated from those numbers, not looked up. The bright box is one conventional unit cell; the faint box is the repeated block. "Space-filling" draws metal atoms with radius = half the nearest-neighbour distance and molecular solids with van der Waals radii.
